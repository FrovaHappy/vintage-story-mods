Seraph QoL (modid: seraphqol)
A unified, modular Quality-of-Life suite for Vintage Story: inventory sorting & protection, alloy calculator, radial menus, quick light & shield equip, hotbar refill, slot status bars, configurable entity health bars, livestock badges, exploration helpers, and tactical wild fauna rebalancing.
Required Dependency: Install Seraph UI (seraphui) alongside this mod. Every module can be enabled, disabled, or tuned individually via ConfigLib or the .json config files.
Included Modules
Module Description
Inventory & Containers Title-bar buttons to Compact & Sort (scroll mouse wheel over the button to cycle Category / Name / Code / Count), Quick Store into open containers, Store Nearby (Shift+G, respects land claims), Favorites, Locked Slots, optional Force-Stack Perishables, and matching item slot highlights.
Alloy Calculator Attaches automatically next to firepits holding a fired crucible. Choose any alloy or pure metal, plan exact waste-free nugget counts and melt temperatures, estimate fuel, and deposit nuggets + fuel in one click (optionally scanning nearby containers).
Radial Menus, Quick Light & Shield Replaces the Tool Mode grid (F) with a Vintage Story beveled radial wheel (supports mouse wheel paging when >10 modes), adds a Quick-Swap Radial (Alt+F, supports both tap-to-click and hold-to-release), Equip Brightest Light (Alt+L), and Equip Best Shield (Alt+B) to equip your highest-absorption shield into offhand/hotbar and toggle back to your previous item.
Hotbar Refill Automatically refills depleted blocks and consumables on your hotbar from unlocked backpack slots, with optional preventive tool swap before breaking, configurable delay, stack preference, and item blacklist.
Slot Status Indicators Compact bars on inventory slots for food freshness (pulses red when spoiling soon), liquid fill level, crucible molten metal, meal servings, preparation progress, clothing condition, and night-vision temporal fuel.
Exploration & Tracking Ground Item Highlight (hold Shift to highlight nearby dropped items and labels), Resource Particles (resin logs, Terra Preta, tall sparks above buried meteoric iron), and Projectile Tracker (temporary map marker where arrows/spears land).
Entity Health Bars, Livestock Badges & Fauna Rebalance Displays retro-framed Entity Health Bars configurable per entity type (Hostiles & Predators, Passive Fauna & Livestock, Traders & NPCs, Other Players, plus Only When Damaged, Require Sneak, and range). Hold Shift near animals to stack separate square badges for Sex (♂/♀), Pregnancy (+), and Generation (I, II, III...) neatly above the health bar. Includes server-side wild animal HP & butchering drop scaling with 5 presets (Balanced Tactical, Vanilla, Realistic Hunt, Abundant Resources, Hardcore Predators).
Default Hotkeys
Shift + G Store Nearby — deposit matching backpack items into nearby containers
F / Alt + F Tool Mode Radial Wheel (F) & Quick-Swap Tool/Light Radial (Alt+F)
Alt + L Equip / restore the brightest light source in your offhand (hotbar keybind also available in Controls)
Alt + B Equip / restore the best shield in your offhand (hotbar keybind also available in Controls)
Shift (Sneak) Highlight nearby ground items & show animal sex/pregnancy/generation square badges above health bars
Configuration Files
Client preferences are saved to ModConfig/seraphqol.json and server rules to ModConfig/seraphqol-server.json. Both support editing via ConfigLib and direct .json configuration.
